THE PEPSI BOT
Murphy Roszak & Connor Homes
Milwaukee School of Engineering | MEC 1910-185 | May 2026

CHALLENGE
Build a robot that locates targets, drives toward them, detects contact, and moves to a different target.

SYSTEM
Arduino Uno, servo-mounted ultrasonic distance sensor, motor-driven chassis, mechanical bumper switches, LEDs, and control logic developed with MATLAB.

CONTROL SEQUENCE
Scan the environment, select the closest target, turn and verify alignment, drive until bumper contact, update an LED, reverse, and search for a different target.

MURPHY'S CONTRIBUTIONS
The presentation's task tracker lists the steering function, scanning function, and plotting system under Murphy. Conversion to C and the final presentation were shared. Connor led robot electronics and bumper work.

ITERATION
The first contact detector used a photoresistor but proved unreliable under changing ambient light. Two mechanical buttons, springs, and rubber bands provided a more dependable bumper mechanism.

REPORTED RESULTS
The demonstration run contacted every target in the course.
Across broader testing, the reported completion rate was approximately 60%.
A scan took approximately 18 seconds.

NEXT IMPROVEMENTS
Reduce scanning time and aggregate repeated readings at each angle.
